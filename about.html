About BidSpot

The bidding house for website traffic

BidSpot.in is the auction house for website visibility and digital attention. Built to deliver direct, high-intent traffic without complex ad networks or opaque algorithms, BidSpot lets creators, founders, and startups bid for top floor visibility spots in real time.

How the auction operates

  1. Bidders place floor bids starting at ₹100 (or $1+).
  2. The highest bid claims Rank #1 — top floor visibility.
  3. Runner-up bids claim Rank #2, Rank #3, and sequential floor spots.
  4. Outbids update floor placements live across every connected view.

Why a bidding leaderboard

Most ad platforms hide their pricing behind algorithms you can't see or influence. BidSpot is the opposite: the price to be #1 is exactly what the current #1 bidder paid, plus whatever you're willing to add. No auction house, no black box — just a transparent, real-time price for attention.

Who it's for

Indie hackers launching a new product, startups wanting a burst of qualified traffic, and creators promoting a handle or portfolio — anyone who'd rather pay a known price for visibility than guess at an ad platform's algorithm.
