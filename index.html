About BidSpot Auction House

The Bidding House for Website Traffic
BidSpot.in is the premier auction house for website visibility and digital attention. Built to deliver direct high-intent traffic without complex ad networks or opaque algorithms, BidSpot lets creators, founders, and startups bid for top floor visibility spots in real-time.

How the Auction Operates:
1. Bidders place floor bids starting at $1+ (₹100).
2. The highest bid claims Rank #1 (Top Floor visibility).
3. Runner-up bids claim Rank #2, Rank #3, and sequential floor spots.
4. Outbids update floor placements live across all connected catalog views!

House Guidelines

  • Transparent Rankings: Spots are strictly ordered by total floor bid amount in descending order.
  • Open 24/7: There's no bidding session or cut-off time — place a bid whenever you like and it ranks the moment payment clears.
  • Real-time Outbidding: Competitors can place higher floor bids at any time. When outbid, rank positions shift instantly.
  • Settlement: All bids are final upon payment confirmation.
