House Rules

How ranking, bidding, and categories work

Ranking

  • Spots are strictly ordered by total bid amount, descending — no manual curation of rank order.
  • Rank changes take effect immediately once a payment is verified.
  • Minimum bid is ₹100.
  • Ties: if two listings reach the same total, the one that was listed earlier keeps the higher rank. To overtake it, place a bid that exceeds its total — matching the total is not enough.

Boards

  • All-Time Board: ranked by cumulative lifetime bids. Positions are permanent until outbid.
  • Today's Board: ranked by bids placed in the current 24-hour cycle; totals reset daily.
  • Hall of Fame: the top bidder overall and per category each week, snapshotted automatically every Monday.

Categories

Every listing belongs to one of our categories, from AI Agents & Infrastructure and Developer Tools to Crypto, Web3 & Investing, and many more — see the full category list. Category placement is set at submission.

Listing review

  • A new listing goes live automatically the moment its first bid is paid and verified — there's no waiting period.
  • Listings that violate the Terms of Service — illegal content, malware, phishing, or infringing material — will be removed if reported or discovered, even after payment; see the Refund Policy for what happens to that bid.
  • Verified badges are granted at our discretion for established, recognizable brands.
  • Founding Bidder badges are awarded automatically to the first 20 listings that ever clear a payment on BidSpot. It's permanent, can't be bought, and is never granted by hand — it only records that a listing was one of the first on the board.

Re-bidding your own listing

You can bid on your own site as many times as you like. Because we allow only one listing per website, a new bid on a site that's already on the board is added to that listing's existing total rather than creating a second listing — so ₹500 followed by ₹700 puts you at ₹1,200 and ranks you accordingly. Bidding on a site already listed by someone else tops up their listing in the same way; to compete against it instead, list your own site and outbid their total.

Fair use

  • One listing per website/handle. Duplicate listings for the same destination may be merged or removed.
  • Bots, scripted bidding, or payment manipulation will result in listing removal without refund.
  • All bids are final once payment is confirmed — see the Refund & Cancellation Policy for the exceptions.
